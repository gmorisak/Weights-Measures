The Science of Measuring for Baking

Why Weighing Beats Measuring by Volume: Flour is compressible. Depending on how packed the cup is, a cup of flour can weigh anywhere between 115 grams and 160 grams—a variation of over 30%! That is the number one cause of dense cakes, dry cookies, and heavy bread.

Fluff-and-Spoon vs. Dip-and-Sweep:

  • King Arthur Baking: Recommends the fluff-and-spoon method (fluff the flour in the bin, spoon it gently into the cup, then sweep level with a flat edge). This yields 120 grams per cup.
  • LA Times Test Kitchen: Tests recipes using the dip-and-sweep method (dipping the measuring cup directly into the bag/container and sweeping level). This compresses the flour, yielding 142 grams per cup (18% heavier).

This app provides both authoritative standards side-by-side so your baked goods turn out exactly as intended by the recipe author.
